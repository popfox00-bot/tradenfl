Position-adjusted trade analyzer
10-team · 15-round · dynasty draft board
Values are a position-adjusted model derived from FantasyPros consensus rank: each player's overall rank is converted to a value relative to a replacement baseline at their own position (QB 16 / RB 34 / WR 40 / TE 15), then weighted for positional scarcity — so a TE7 or RB19 is scored against other TEs and RBs, not buried by flat overall rank. Starting-lineup impact assumes a 1 QB · 2 RB · 2 WR · 1 TE · 1 FLEX · 1 K · 1 DEF lineup and compares each team's best possible starters before and after the deal.
