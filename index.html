Value is driven primarily by each player's overall consensus rank (FantasyPros ECR), converted through a decay curve so being a WR7 overall means meaningfully more than being an RB7 overall by rank alone — a modest position-scarcity multiplier (RB and TE run slightly hotter, since they're scarcer at the replacement level) and a starter-tier premium then nudge value up for players who'd actually crack a typical 10-team starting lineup, and down for pure bench depth, since bench production doesn't score points. Starting-lineup impact assumes a 1 QB · 2 RB · 2 WR · 1 TE · 1 FLEX · 1 K · 1 DEF lineup and compares each team's best possible starters before and after the deal. Chain paths are two independent, sequential trades — the first happens on its own merits between two other teams, the second is your follow-up once they've actually made it — not a single combined transaction.
