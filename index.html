Trade Magic

Set what you'd move and who you want. The engine always searches for the deal that costs you the least — it protects your two most valuable block pieces by default and only offers them up if nothing cheaper gets accepted — then checks direct offers first, then a two-step chain: another team acquires your target from its current owner in a fair deal of its own (sometimes bundled with a depth piece to free up a log-jam), and only then do you flip something from your block to get it from them.

Power Rankings

Ranked 1–10 by optimal starting-lineup value — the same metric used everywhere else in this tool — recalculated live as rosters and trades change. Playoff odds and projected record come from a Monte Carlo simulation of a 13-game regular season using each team's overall strength; since this tool doesn't have your league's actual week-by-week schedule, the simulation randomizes matchups across thousands of trials and assumes a top-6 playoff format, common for 10-team leagues.
Value is driven primarily by each player's overall consensus rank (FantasyPros ECR), converted through a decay curve so being a WR7 overall means meaningfully more than being an RB7 overall by rank alone — a modest position-scarcity multiplier (RB and TE run slightly hotter, since they're scarcer at the replacement level) and a starter-tier premium then nudge value up for players who'd actually crack a typical 10-team starting lineup, and down for pure bench depth, since bench production doesn't score points. A small early-season schedule adjustment (±5%, skill positions only) is also folded in for teams with a clearly favorable or unfavorable Weeks 1–4 slate per current research — the idea being a hot, easy-schedule start is a sell-high window, not a reason to overhaul a valuation. Starting-lineup impact assumes a 1 QB · 2 RB · 2 WR · 1 TE · 1 FLEX · 1 K · 1 DEF lineup and compares each team's best possible starters before and after the deal. Chain paths are sequential, independent trades — the first happens on its own merits between two other teams, the next is your follow-up once it's actually happened — not a single combined transaction. Roster edits and completed trades are saved in this browser only (via local storage) — they won't sync across devices, and clearing your browser data will reset them.
